Comparison FAQs

Which flooring is cheapest over 20 years?

LVP at ~$7,700 for 1,000 sq ft — it lasts the full 20 years with no replacement. Laminate runs ~$8,433 (one replacement); carpet ~$11,000 (one replacement). Solid hardwood costs most upfront ($16,775) but least per year (~$0.22/sq ft/yr).

Is tile or LVP cheaper for a kitchen?

LVP: ~$1,540 installed for 200 sq ft. Tile: ~$2,695. Per year, tile wins (~$54/yr vs ~$77/yr) because it lasts 2–3× longer. Choose by budget horizon and DIY plans.

Why is tile labor so expensive?

Thinset mortar work, precision cutting around fixtures, and grout — usually across multiple visits with cure time between. At $4–$10/sq ft, tile labor routinely exceeds the tile's own material cost. Always get tile labor quoted separately.
