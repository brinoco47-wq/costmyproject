Asphalt vs. Metal Roof Cost: The 30-Year Head-to-Head

CostMyProject Research Team Prices checked October 2026

The most common roofing question we see: "is metal worth double the price?" We priced both roofs on the exact same house, itemized every line — then ran the clock forward 30 years. The answer surprised us too.

The 30-year amortization

Divide each total by its rated lifespan and the ranking inverts:

Architectural asphaltStanding-seam metal
Installed total$17,120$33,890
Rated lifespan (mid)27.5 years55 years
Cost per year of rated life$623/yr$616/yr
Installs needed in 30 years1.09 (second roof at ~yr 27.5)0.55 (one roof, with room to spare)
30-yr prorated cost + maintenance*$19,276$18,485
Roof life remaining at year 30~25 yrs (on roof #2)~25 yrs (on roof #1)

* Prorated: total × (30 ÷ lifespan) + maintenance estimates ($600 shingles / ~$0 metal). Shingle math assumes you actually replace at ~27.5 years — which is the part everyone forgets to budget.

Here's the uncomfortable truth for shingle fans: metal is cheaper per year from day one — not after some distant break-even point, immediately. The entire premium is a financing problem, not a value problem. And at year 30, the shingle owner is on roof #2 while the metal owner still hasn't replaced anything.

When asphalt still wins

  • You're selling within 10–15 years. You won't own the replacement; buy the cheaper roof and let the next owner decide.
  • Cash flow is the constraint. $16,770 extra today is real money. A roof you can afford beats a roof you can't.
  • Your HOA or historic district mandates shingles. Some do. Check before dreaming.

When metal wins

  • You're staying 20+ years. The per-year math favors metal immediately and the gap only widens.
  • Hail, wildfire, or heavy snow. Metal's durability isn't theoretical — it's the difference between a claim and a shrug.
  • You can capture the insurance discount. In hail/fire zones the premium savings compound over 55 years.
  • You hate re-roofing. One and done has real lifestyle value.

Asphalt vs. metal FAQs

How much more does a metal roof cost than asphalt shingles?

About 98% more upfront at standard tiers: ~$33,890 vs. ~$17,120 on the same 2,000-sqft-footprint home (2,236 sqft of roof) — a $16,770 difference at October 2026 prices. Run your own numbers in the roof cost calculator.

Is a metal roof worth the extra cost?

Per year of service, yes — ~$616/yr vs. ~$623/yr for architectural shingles, cheaper from day one. Over 30 years the prorated cost is ~$18,485 vs. ~$19,276, and the metal roof still has ~25 years of life left at year 30.

Are metal roofs noisy in the rain?

No. Residential standing-seam installs over solid decking, underlayment, and attic insulation — rain noise is roughly the same as shingles. The noisy version is uninsulated metal over open purlins, i.e., a barn.

Can you install a metal roof over shingles?

Sometimes — one existing layer can stay underneath in many jurisdictions, saving ~$1.50/sqft in tear-off. But you can't inspect the decking and the trapped layer adds weight. Full tear-off is the better roof; overlay is the cheaper one.

Does a metal roof lower homeowners insurance?

Often, especially in hail and wildfire zones where the Class A fire rating and impact resistance earn real discounts. Get the insurance quote before choosing — the savings compound over a 55-year roof life.
