Moving costs in Georgia

CostMyProject Research Team Prices checked October 2026

Moving in Georgia? Local moves run $1,200–$2,500 for a 2-bedroom home, while long-distance moves price by weight and distance — a 2-bedroom going 1,000+ miles runs $3,500–$6,500. Below: the full tables, what drives Georgia prices, and how to pay less.

The 30-second version Under 100 miles, Georgia movers bill by the hour (2-mover crews ~$80–$100/hr nationally, higher in Georgia's big metros). Over 100 miles, it's flat weight-and-distance pricing under federal FMCSA rules. Georgia moves price right at national averages in our model — see the methodology note below.

Worked example: 2-bedroom local move in Georgia

How the table range is built — a 2-bedroom apartment moving across Atlanta (~25 miles):

  • Crew: 3 movers × ~7 hours (load, drive, unload)
  • Effective all-in rate: ~$88/hr (crew + truck + standard equipment, Georgia-adjusted)
  • Estimated total: 21 crew-hours × $88 ≈ $1,850 — the midpoint of the $1,200–$2,500 range above
  • Your planning range (±25%): $1,400–$2,300

Stairs, long carries, packing services, or a month-end date push you toward the top of the range; a ground-floor unit with everything boxed and a mid-month date pulls toward the bottom. This is a budgeting estimate, not a quote — confirm with a binding written estimate.

What drives the price in Georgia

  • Atlanta traffic inflates hourly jobs — A crosstown Atlanta move can add an hour or more of paid drive time. Schedule around rush hour — a 10am start beats a 4pm start on your invoice.
  • Summer heat peak — May–August is both hottest and busiest. Crews slow in 95°F heat; morning starts are worth insisting on.
  • Storm season — Tropical systems can sideswipe Georgia moves June–November. Less direct risk than Florida, but delays happen.
  • Sprawl tax — Metro Atlanta's footprint means 'local' moves with real mileage. Confirm how drive time is billed.
  • Competitive, healthy market — Atlanta's deep mover bench keeps pricing near national averages — shopping 3+ quotes genuinely pays here.

How to save money on a Georgia move

  1. Start Atlanta moves early morning to dodge both heat and traffic.
  2. Winter (Dec–Feb) is the cheapest window — mild weather, low demand.
  3. Declutter before quoting: long-distance Georgia moves price by weight.
  4. Avoid month-end in Atlanta's rental-heavy neighborhoods if you can.
  5. If you're moving inside metro Atlanta, ask whether the quote includes drive time between locations — some carriers bill it, some don't, and the difference is real money on crosstown jobs.

FAQs

What is the cheapest month to move in Georgia?

December through February. Demand drops, weather stays workable, and carriers discount — Atlanta winters rarely stop a move.

How does Atlanta traffic affect moving costs?

Local moves are hourly, so traffic is literally billable. A bad traffic window can add $100–$300+ to a job — schedule mid-morning starts and avoid rush hour.

How much does it cost to move from Georgia to Florida?

A short interstate hop (~350 miles). For a 2-bedroom, expect low-four-figures full-service (see table) — well under the 1,000-mile national ranges.

Do movers work in Georgia summer heat?

Yes — but insist on a morning start. Afternoon jobs in July/August run slower, and reputable crews plan water and break time into the day.

Should I get a binding estimate?

For any interstate move, yes — binding or binding-not-to-exceed, in writing, after a survey. It's your main protection against delivery-day surprises.
