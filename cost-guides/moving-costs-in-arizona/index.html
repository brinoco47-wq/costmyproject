Moving costs in Arizona

CostMyProject Research Team Prices checked October 2026

Moving in Arizona? Local moves run $1,250–$2,600 for a 2-bedroom home, while long-distance moves price by weight and distance — a 2-bedroom going 1,000+ miles runs $3,700–$6,800. Below: the full tables, what drives Arizona prices, and how to pay less.

The 30-second version Under 100 miles, Arizona movers bill by the hour (2-mover crews ~$80–$100/hr nationally, higher in Arizona's big metros). Over 100 miles, it's flat weight-and-distance pricing under federal FMCSA rules. Arizona moves price ~5% above national averages in our model — see the methodology note below.

Worked example: 2-bedroom local move in Arizona

How the table range is built — a 2-bedroom apartment moving across Phoenix (~25 miles):

  • Crew: 3 movers × ~7 hours (load, drive, unload)
  • Effective all-in rate: ~$92/hr (crew + truck + standard equipment, Arizona-adjusted)
  • Estimated total: 21 crew-hours × $92 ≈ $1,925 — the midpoint of the $1,250–$2,600 range above
  • Your planning range (±25%): $1,450–$2,400

Stairs, long carries, packing services, or a month-end date push you toward the top of the range; a ground-floor unit with everything boxed and a mid-month date pulls toward the bottom. This is a budgeting estimate, not a quote — confirm with a binding written estimate.

What drives the price in Arizona

  • Snowbird season inverts pricing — October–April is Arizona's peak — winter visitors flood in, demand spikes. Summer, brutal as it is, is the discount season.
  • Extreme heat reshapes the workday — Phoenix summer moves start at dawn and crews build in heat breaks. Afternoon jobs in July are slower and riskier — morning starts aren't optional, they're safety.
  • Monsoon season — July–September dust storms and flash flooding can delay moves, especially in southern Arizona. Watch the forecast in monsoon months.
  • Phoenix sprawl — The Valley is enormous — crosstown moves carry real drive time. Confirm billing for drive time upfront.
  • Storage needs AC — If you need storage between homes, Arizona summer makes climate-controlled units worth the premium — heat damage to belongings is real.

How to save money on a Arizona move

  1. Summer moves are cheapest — but only with a dawn start time, non-negotiable.
  2. Snowbird season (Oct–Apr) books out; reserve early if those are your dates.
  3. If storing anything over summer, pay for climate control.
  4. Declutter before long hauls — Arizona outbound moves (to CA/TX) price by weight.

FAQs

What is the cheapest month to move in Arizona?

June through August — peak heat, lowest demand. The discount is real, but so is 115°F: insist on the earliest start time and keep water on hand.

Why are winter moves expensive in Arizona?

Snowbird season (October–April) brings hundreds of thousands of seasonal residents. Mover demand — and prices — peak while the rest of the country is in its off-season.

Do Arizona movers work in extreme heat?

Yes, with adjusted schedules: dawn starts, heat breaks, and shorter afternoon windows. Reputable Phoenix crews won't book a 2pm July start — and you shouldn't want them to.

How much does it cost to move from Arizona to California?

A short interstate hop (~370 miles). For a 2-bedroom, expect low-four-figures full-service (see table) — far under cross-country ranges.

Do I need climate-controlled storage in Arizona?

If your belongings sit through summer, yes — heat warps wood, melts adhesives, and ruins electronics. It's one of the few places where climate control isn't optional.
