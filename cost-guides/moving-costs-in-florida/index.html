Moving costs in Florida

CostMyProject Research Team Prices checked October 2026

Moving in Florida? Local moves run $1,300–$2,750 for a 2-bedroom home, while long-distance moves price by weight and distance — a 2-bedroom going 1,000+ miles runs $3,850–$7,150. Below: the full tables, what drives Florida prices, and how to pay less.

The 30-second version Under 100 miles, Florida movers bill by the hour (2-mover crews ~$80–$100/hr nationally, higher in Florida's big metros). Over 100 miles, it's flat weight-and-distance pricing under federal FMCSA rules. Florida moves price ~10% above national averages in our model — see the methodology note below.

Worked example: 2-bedroom local move in Florida

How the table range is built — a 2-bedroom apartment moving across Orlando (~25 miles):

  • Crew: 3 movers × ~7 hours (load, drive, unload)
  • Effective all-in rate: ~$96/hr (crew + truck + standard equipment, Florida-adjusted)
  • Estimated total: 21 crew-hours × $96 ≈ $2,025 — the midpoint of the $1,300–$2,750 range above
  • Your planning range (±25%): $1,500–$2,550

Stairs, long carries, packing services, or a month-end date push you toward the top of the range; a ground-floor unit with everything boxed and a mid-month date pulls toward the bottom. This is a budgeting estimate, not a quote — confirm with a binding written estimate.

What drives the price in Florida

  • Snowbird season inverts the calendar — October–April is Florida's peak: snowbirds arrive, demand spikes, and prices follow. Summer — miserable heat and all — is the discount season, the opposite of most states.
  • Hurricane season overlaps the cheap months — June–November storms can delay or reschedule moves. The August–September discount comes with genuine weather risk — build a buffer into your dates.
  • High-rise condo rules — South Florida and Tampa high-rises routinely require elevator reservations, certificates of insurance, and restricted move hours. Buildings enforce this strictly; your mover can't override it.
  • Heat and humidity slow crews — Summer moves mean slower work and mandatory breaks. It's manageable, but don't expect winter productivity in August.
  • Toll roads on long-distance routes — Florida's turnpike system adds real cost to long hauls — confirm whether tolls are included in your quote or billed extra.

How to save money on a Florida move

  1. Moving May–September? That's Florida's value season — just watch the hurricane forecast.
  2. Book your building's freight elevator the same day you book the mover, not after.
  3. Get the COI (certificate of insurance) sorted a week early — condos reject movers without one.
  4. Ask for a binding estimate; snowbird-season demand makes non-binding quotes drift upward.

FAQs

Why do Florida moving prices spike in winter?

Snowbird season (October–April) floods the state with seasonal residents. Mover demand surges while the rest of the country is in its off-season, so Florida prices peak exactly when other states bottom out.

What is the cheapest month to move in Florida?

August or September — peak heat, peak hurricane risk, lowest demand. If your dates are flexible and you can tolerate the weather gamble, that's the value window.

Do Florida condos really require elevator reservations?

Most high-rises do, plus a certificate of insurance from the mover and restricted hours (often weekdays only). Confirm your building's move policy before getting quotes — it shapes the whole job.

How do hurricanes affect scheduled moves?

Carriers may delay or reschedule around named storms, and delivery windows stretch. Ask about the carrier's weather-delay policy in writing, especially June–November.

Is it cheaper to move to Florida in summer?

Usually yes for the move itself — but weigh it against the weather risk and the fact that you'll be unpacking in 95°F heat. For many people the shoulder months (May, late September) are the sweet spot.
