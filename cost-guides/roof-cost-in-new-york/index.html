Roof Costs in New York (2026)

CostMyProject Research Team Prices checked October 2026

A roof replacement on a standard 2,000 sq ft New York home costs about $19,468 at 6/12 pitch with architectural shingles — $2,348 above the national average of $17,120. The entire difference is labor: New York construction wages run above the national average, while materials, tear-off, and permits cost the same. This page shows the New York math, how it compares to the South average, and what the New York number does not include.

The New York worked example: same roof, New York labor

Same roof as our national example: a 40×50 ft colonial, 2,000 sq ft footprint, 6/12 pitch (roof surface 2,236 sq ft), architectural shingles, one-layer tear-off, $350 permit. Only the labor line changes — scaled by the New York labor multiplier of ×1.30.

So a New York quote roughly $3,209 above a generic "Southern" estimate is not overcharging — it is what the New York wage data predicts. The reverse also holds: a New York quote at the national average deserves a second look at what is missing from it.

How the New York multiplier is calculated

The ×1.30 figure is the New York state average for construction laborers from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics (May 2023), ratioed to the national mean wage — the same dataset behind this site's ZIP-by-ZIP labor adjustment. In our ZIP3 data, 40 New York ZIP3 areas carry the state-average value; New York City metro runs higher than the state average — enter your ZIP in the roof cost calculator for your metro's exact multiplier. Materials are not scaled: shingles cost the same in New York as nationally in this model.

What the New York number does not include

  • Pitch and stories. Steep pitches (+15% at 9/12, +25% at 12/12) and second stories (+10–20%) raise labor on top of the Texas base.
  • Decking replacement. Rotten decking found during tear-off is billed per sheet on top of the quote.
  • A second tear-off layer. The example assumes one layer; two layers roughly double the $3,354 tear-off line.
  • Storm and code effects. The multiplier is labor-only. Snow-load and code requirements show up in individual New York quotes, not in this average.

The honest way to use this page: treat $19,468 as the New York baseline for this exact roof, then adjust for your pitch, stories, layers, and decking — or run your roof in the calculator with your ZIP.

New York roof cost FAQs

Why is New York pricier than the South average?

Because the New York state-average labor multiplier (×1.30) is above the South regional mean (×0.89). The South mean blends higher- and lower-wage states, so a New York-specific figure lands higher. The gap is $3,209 on the example roof, all labor.

How is the New York multiplier calculated?

From BLS OEWS May 2023 mean wages for construction laborers: the New York state average ratioed to the national mean. 40 New York ZIP3 areas in our data carry the state-average value (×1.30).

Does the New York estimate include storm or code costs?

No. The multiplier adjusts labor only; materials, tear-off, and permit are national figures. Snow-load and code requirements appear in individual quotes — get at least two local New York quotes before deciding.
