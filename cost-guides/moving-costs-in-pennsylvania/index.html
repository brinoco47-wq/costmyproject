Moving costs in Pennsylvania

CostMyProject Research Team Prices checked October 2026

Moving in Pennsylvania? Local moves run $1,150–$2,400 for a 2-bedroom home, while long-distance moves price by weight and distance — a 2-bedroom going 1,000+ miles runs $3,300–$6,200. Below: the full tables, what drives Pennsylvania prices, and how to pay less.

The 30-second version Under 100 miles, Pennsylvania movers bill by the hour (2-mover crews ~$80–$100/hr nationally, higher in Pennsylvania's big metros). Over 100 miles, it's flat weight-and-distance pricing under federal FMCSA rules. Pennsylvania moves price ~5% below national averages in our model — see the methodology note below.

Worked example: 2-bedroom local move in Pennsylvania

How the table range is built — a 2-bedroom apartment moving across Philadelphia (~25 miles):

  • Crew: 3 movers × ~7 hours (load, drive, unload)
  • Effective all-in rate: ~$85/hr (crew + truck + standard equipment, Pennsylvania-adjusted)
  • Estimated total: 21 crew-hours × $85 ≈ $1,775 — the midpoint of the $1,150–$2,400 range above
  • Your planning range (±25%): $1,350–$2,200

Stairs, long carries, packing services, or a month-end date push you toward the top of the range; a ground-floor unit with everything boxed and a mid-month date pulls toward the bottom. This is a budgeting estimate, not a quote — confirm with a binding written estimate.

What drives the price in Pennsylvania

  • Philly rowhomes and narrow streets — South Philly and similar neighborhoods often can't fit a full-size truck — expect shuttle fees (small truck transfers) if your street is tight. Mention it when quoting.
  • Pittsburgh terrain — Hills, steps, and multi-level homes add carry time. Pittsburgh moves price the topography in whether the quote says so or not.
  • Winter is the value season — December–February is cheapest — but snow and ice can delay jobs. The discount is real; so is the weather.
  • Turnpike tolls on long hauls — PA Turnpike tolls add up on long-distance routes. Confirm whether tolls are included in your estimate.
  • Older housing stock — Narrow stairwells and small doorways in pre-war homes slow crews down — another quiet hours-adder on local jobs.

How to save money on a Pennsylvania move

  1. Winter moves are cheapest in Pennsylvania — just keep a weather buffer.
  2. Ask explicitly about shuttle fees if you're in a rowhome neighborhood.
  3. End-of-month books first in Philly and Pittsburgh; mid-month is calmer and cheaper.
  4. For Philly-to-NYC/NJ corridors, compare carriers on both sides of the state line.

FAQs

What is the cheapest month to move in Pennsylvania?

December through February. Demand is lowest and carriers discount, though snow can force rescheduling — keep dates flexible.

What is a shuttle fee?

When a full-size moving truck can't reach your home (narrow Philly streets, for example), the crew shuttles your belongings in a smaller truck. It's a legitimate charge — but it should be disclosed in the quote, not sprung on move day.

How much does a Philly local move cost?

A 2-bedroom local move in Philadelphia typically falls in the low-to-mid four figures (see the table). Narrow-street shuttle fees and parking are the Philly-specific wildcards.

Do Pennsylvania movers charge for stairs?

Most bill the extra time rather than a separate fee, but steep Pittsburgh hillsides and walk-ups measurably add hours. Disclose access details upfront.

Is a binding estimate worth it for long-distance?

Yes — especially on popular corridors like PA-to-Florida. Binding or binding-not-to-exceed estimates lock your price; non-binding ones can grow on delivery day.
