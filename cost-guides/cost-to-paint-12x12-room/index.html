Cost to paint a 12×12 room

Step 1: the wall math (348 sq ft)

This is the part everyone skips — and the reason paint estimates go wrong. A 12×12 room is not 144 sq ft of painting. You're painting walls, and walls are measured in wall square feet:

Two rooms that are both "12×12" can differ by 50+ sq ft of wall once you count doors, windows, and closets. Always measure your actual room — the 348 number is a starting point, not your number. (For the full measuring method including stairs and odd shapes, see how to measure square footage.)

The third-gallon rule Buy 2 gallons for a standard repaint in a similar color. Buy 3 if you're going dark-to-light, light-to-dark, or the walls are currently unpainted drywall (primer + 2 coats). An unopened gallon returns to the store; running out mid-wall does not.

The paint itself is only part of the DIY bill. Budget another $60–$120 for supplies if you're starting from scratch: roller covers ($10–$20 for good ones — they matter more than the paint brand), an angled sash brush ($12–$18), painter's tape ($8–$12), drop cloths ($15–$30, reusable), a paint tray, and spackle + sandpaper for wall repair. Buy the good roller covers; cheap ones shed lint into your finish.

Primer adds $25–$40 per gallon and one full extra coat of labor. You need it for: bare drywall, dark-to-light changes, stained or patched walls, and glossy existing finishes. You don't need it for a same-color refresh over sound paint — that's the most common wasted gallon in DIY painting.

A 12×12 bedroom is the single best DIY painting value in the house: one weekend saves $600–$1,300, the room is small enough to finish before fatigue ruins your cut-in, and mistakes are cheap to fix. Hire the pro when: ceilings are over 10 ft, there's significant wall repair, you're on a deadline (selling the house), or it's one room of a whole-house job where the crew's day rate makes per-room pricing silly. What changes the number

  • Ceiling height. 9-ft ceilings add ~48 sq ft of wall (+14%); 10-ft add ~96 sq ft (+25%) — plus ladder time, so pro quotes rise faster than the footage.
  • Color change. Dark-to-light typically needs primer + 2–3 coats: +$150–$300 DIY, +30–50% on a pro quote.
  • Wall condition. Heavy patching, wallpaper removal, or smoke damage can double prep time — the #1 source of quote variance on "simple" rooms.
  • Accent wall. One wall in a second color adds ~$40–$80 DIY (a quart or gallon + extra masking) or $100–$250 pro.
  • Closet. A standard reach-in closet adds ~$50–$100 DIY or $100–$200 pro — tight quarters, shelf removal, awkward angles.

Money-saving moves that don't hurt the finish

You don't need the premium gallon to get a premium-looking room — you need the right allocation. Spend on the roller covers and the sash brush (the tools touch every square foot), buy mid-range paint (the quality jump from budget to mid is the biggest in the store), and do the prep yourself even if you hire the rolling out — patching and sanding is unskilled labor billed at skilled rates. The other lever is timing: painters' slow season (late fall through winter, excluding holidays) routinely brings 10–20% off-peak discounts, because a crew they'd rather keep busy beats an empty schedule. Finally, if you're painting multiple rooms, buy the 5-gallon bucket of your main color — the per-gallon price drops 10–15% and touch-ups stay consistent across rooms.

FAQs

How much does it cost to paint a 12×12 room?

$250–$900 DIY or $800–$2,400 professionally in 2026 (planning ranges). The room has about 348 sq ft of paintable wall; pros charge $1–$6 per sq ft of wall area depending on market and prep.

How many gallons of paint for a 12×12 room?

About 2 gallons for two coats (696 sq ft of coverage needed at 350 sq ft/gallon). Buy a third gallon for dark-to-light changes, bare drywall, or just peace of mind — unopened gallons go back to the store.

How long does it take to paint a 12×12 room?

A pro crew finishes in 4–8 hours. DIY takes a full weekend: one day for prep, patching, and cutting in; one day for two rolled coats with dry time between. Add a day for primer or dramatic color changes.

Is it cheaper to paint a room yourself?

Dramatically — $140–$240 in materials versus $800–$1,600 for a pro on the same 12×12 room. It's one of the best DIY returns in home improvement: a weekend of work saves $600–$1,300.

Does ceiling height change the cost to paint a room?

Yes. 9-ft ceilings add ~14% more wall area versus 8-ft; 10-ft add ~25%. Pro quotes rise faster than the raw footage because of ladder work and slower cut-in — budget an extra 20–35% for tall walls.
