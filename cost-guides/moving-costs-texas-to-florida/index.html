Moving from Texas to Florida? A 3-bedroom home costs about $7,274 full-service over the 1,364-mile route using our calculator's published formula — $5,010 by container, $2,100 driving a rental truck. Below: prices by home size, how the math works, what Florida costs on arrival, and how to time it.

How the math works

Our long-distance formula is weight x ($0.50 + $0.0003 x miles), with a $1,200 minimum that does not bind anywhere on this route. At 1,364 miles the distance factor is $0.50 + $0.4092 = $0.9092 per pound. So a 3-bedroom home at 8,000 lb is 8,000 x $0.9092 = $7,273.60, rounded to $7,274. Container pricing is the home-size base plus $2.50 per mile (1,364 x $2.50 = $3,410 of the container column). DIY one-way truck pricing is a base plus a per-mile rate by truck size: 10-foot $400 + $0.85/mile, 15-foot $500 + $1.00/mile, 20-foot $600 + $1.10/mile, 26-foot $700 + $1.25/mile. Every figure in the table above can be reproduced from these inputs — that is the point of publishing the formula.

Worked example: 3-bedroom Texas to Florida

Full-service line-haul: 8,000 lb x $0.9092 = $7,274. Container: $1,600 base + $3,410 mileage = $5,010. DIY 20-foot truck: $600 base + 1,364 x $1.10 = $600 + $1,500.40 = $2,100. Add full packing from our model ($1,615 for a 3-bedroom, boxes and materials included) and the full-service job becomes $7,274 + $1,615 = $8,889. Supplies alone are $275 if you pack yourself. The cash gap between DIY and full-service on this route is $7,274 - $2,100 = $5,174 before packing — the question is what your driving days are worth, not whether the truck is cheaper.

What drives the price on this route

  • Weight first. Every 1,000 lb adds $909 at this distance. Decluttering before the weigh-in is the highest-leverage saving on a long-distance move.
  • Distance is fixed. You cannot negotiate the miles — but you can choose the service type, and the table shows containers beating full-service by roughly $2,000+ at 3 bedrooms and up.
  • Season. Summer weekends and end-of-month dates are peak; see the timing section below.
  • Access. Stairs, elevators, long carries, and shuttle service are the classic add-ons — get them named in writing, not discovered on moving day.
  • Packing. Full packing for a 3-bedroom is $1,615 in our model; fragile-only runs at 40% of full packing.

Released value: the $24 television warning

Every interstate quote includes federal released value at 60 cents per pound — and that is almost nothing. A 40-pound television settles at $24 if it is destroyed. Full-value protection costs extra, typically 1 to 2 percent of the declared value as reported in route add-on tables. If you own anything you cannot afford to replace at 60 cents a pound, get the valuation basis named in writing on your quote before you sign.

When to move: timing on this route

Typical industry-reported adjustments (not our measurements — mover pricing is seasonal everywhere): end-of-month dates add 10 to 15 percent; weekends add 5 to 8 percent; mid-month, mid-week moves in the off-season from October through April run 20 to 30 percent cheaper than peak, per our own moving guide. Two route-specific wrinkles: summer is peak season nationally, and Florida's snowbird season from October through April pushes Florida demand up exactly when the rest of the country cools off — so the off-season discount is real but smaller on the Florida end than on a purely northern route.

Texas to Florida moving quote checklist

  • Binding vs non-binding — a binding quote cannot rise unless you add items; a non-binding quote can.
  • Valuation basis — released value (60 cents/lb) vs full-value protection, named in writing.
  • Weight tickets — ask how weight is verified on a long-distance job.
  • All fees in writing — stairs, elevator, long carry, shuttle, storage-in-transit, fuel surcharge.
  • FMCSA interstate authority — verify the carrier's USDOT number before booking.
  • Delivery window, not just a date — long-distance delivery is a range; get it in writing.

FAQs

How much does it cost to move from Texas to Florida?

For a 3-bedroom home over the 1,364-mile route, about $7,274 full-service, $5,010 by container, or $2,100 driving a rental truck, using our calculator formula. A studio runs about $1,637 full-service; a 5-bedroom about $13,638. Packing is extra: full packing for a 3-bedroom is $1,615 in our model.

How long does a Texas to Florida move take?

Carriers quote a delivery window, not a single day, for a 1,364-mile interstate move. Get the window in writing before booking, and ask what happens if the truck misses it. Delivery windows stretch around summer peak and winter weather.

Is it cheaper to drive a rental truck from Texas to Florida?

On cash alone, yes: a one-way 20-foot truck for a 3-bedroom home is about $2,100 in our model versus $7,274 full-service. The gap is your time: 1,364 miles is two to three days of driving plus hotels and meals. If those cost you more than the gap, hiring movers wins.

What is the cheapest time to move from Texas to Florida?

Mid-month, mid-week, in the off-season from October through April runs about 20 to 30 percent cheaper than peak in our guide. Avoid end-of-month dates, which typically add 10 to 15 percent, and summer weekends.

Does moving insurance cover everything?

No. Federal released value covers only 60 cents per pound, so a 40-pound television settles at 24 dollars. Full-value protection costs extra, typically 1 to 2 percent of the declared value. Get the valuation basis named in writing on your quote.
