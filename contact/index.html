Contact

The fastest way to reach us is email. We read everything; corrections and calculator bug reports get answered first.

Email: hello@costmyproject.me

Tip: include the page URL and, for price corrections, a link to the current listing or quote. That lets us verify and update the same day.

What to send us

  • Wrong or stale prices — top priority, jumps the queue.
  • Calculator bugs — tell us your inputs and what looked wrong.
  • New calculator requests — we build one tool at a time; votes genuinely influence the order.
  • Contractor or retailer? — we're happy to hear about systematic pricing data, but paid placement never changes our numbers. See the disclosure.

We don't offer personalized quotes or contractor referrals — the calculators are the service. Response time is typically a few days.
