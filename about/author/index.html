CostMyProject Research Team

Independent researchers · Publishing since September 2026

We're the people behind every calculator and cost guide on this site. We're independent researchers focused on one thing: what home projects actually cost, backed by dated numbers you can verify.

How we work

Every price on this site goes through the same process:

  • We collect current prices from manufacturer spec sheets, retailer listings, Bureau of Labor Statistics wage data, and industry reports like the JLC Cost vs. Value report.
  • We date-stamp everything. Every page shows when its prices were last checked. Stale numbers are worse than no numbers.
  • We show the math. Our calculators don't hide behind a single magic number — each one breaks the estimate into line items so you can see exactly where the money goes.
  • We use ranges, not false precision. Costs vary by region and project, so we give ±25% planning ranges instead of pretending a single figure fits everyone.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell leads or share your information — there are no contact forms harvesting phone numbers.
  • We don't take payments from contractors to rank them or their prices.
  • We don't publish a number we can't source or explain.

Corrections

Find a price that's wrong or outdated? Email sanaullahx51@gmail.com — corrections jump the queue, and substantive fixes are noted on the page. Our full process is documented in our methodology and editorial policy.
