Privacy Policy

Effective: September 23, 2026

Short version: our calculators run entirely in your browser. We don't ask for your email, we don't sell data, and we collect the minimum needed to keep the site running.

What we collect

  • Calculator inputs stay on your device. Everything you type into a calculator is processed locally in your browser via JavaScript. It is never sent to our servers.
  • Basic server logs. Our hosting provider logs standard technical data (IP address, pages requested, timestamps) for security and reliability. We don't use it to profile visitors.
  • Analytics (planned). We intend to add privacy-respecting analytics to understand which tools people use, so we know what to build next. This policy will be updated before any analytics goes live, and it will not include cross-site tracking.
  • Emails you send us. If you contact us, we keep your message to respond. We don't add you to any list — there is no list.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no email gates, no newsletters you didn't ask for.
  • We don't sell, rent, or share personal information with data brokers.
  • We don't run fingerprinting or cross-site tracking scripts.

Cookies

We don't set any cookies ourselves today. If embedded content or future analytics sets cookies, this section will describe exactly which and why.

Children

This site is a general-audience reference. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Your rights & contact

To ask what data we hold about you, or to request deletion, email hello@costmyproject.me. We'll respond within 30 days.

Changes

We'll update the effective date above when this policy changes, and note material changes here for 30 days.
