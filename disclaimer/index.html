CostMyProject exists to help you budget home projects with honest numbers. This page states, in plain language, what our numbers are — and what they are not.

Estimates, not quotes

Every cost on this site is an approximate budgeting estimate, not a quote, bid, offer, or contract. Calculator results, price ranges, tables and figures are computed from published price data, retailer listings and market research, as described in our methodology. Real project costs vary by location, material grade, site conditions, contractor and market changes after publication. Always get written quotes from licensed local contractors before committing to a project.

Content here is general information about home-project costs. It is not construction, engineering, electrical, plumbing, legal or financial advice, and it does not replace a licensed professional who has inspected your property. Work involving structure, gas, electrical panels or roofing can be dangerous and is often regulated — hire licensed professionals for it.
