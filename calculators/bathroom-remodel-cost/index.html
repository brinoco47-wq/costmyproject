What this calculator estimates

This tool prices a full bathroom remodel from the decisions that actually move the number: your bathroom's size, the scope (refresh through luxury), tub/shower choice, toilet, vanity, tile material, fixture package, whether you're moving plumbing, and your region's labor rates. Every result shows the line-by-line math and a ±25% planning range — because this is a budgeting estimate, not a quote, and real bids move with what's behind the walls and local labor markets.

How the math works

  • Tub / shower: installed flat — keep $0; tub-shower combo $2,500; walk-in tiled shower $6,000; soaker tub $4,500.
  • Toilet: installed — keep $0; standard $450; comfort height $650; bidet-seat combo $1,200.
  • Vanity: installed with top — keep $0; single stock $800; single semi-custom $1,800; double $3,500.
  • Tile: tile area ≈ bathroom sq ft × 2.5 (floor + tub/shower surround walls), installed per sq ft — ceramic $10, porcelain $14, natural stone $22.
  • Fixtures: faucet, showerhead, lighting, mirror, hardware — basic $500, mid-range $1,200, premium $2,500.
  • Plumbing / electrical / drywall: flat allowance by scope — refresh $800, mid-range $2,500, major $5,000, luxury $10,000. Layout change adds $4,000.
  • Labor: 30% (refresh) / 35% (mid) / 40% (major) / 45% (luxury) of the material subtotal, multiplied by your region rate (0.85–1.2).

Every figure is an October 2026 US planning estimate. Real quotes vary — that's why results show a ±25% planning range. This is a budgeting estimate, not a quote.

How to save $5,000 on your bathroom remodel

  • Keep the layout. Moving a toilet means rerouting a 3–4" drain through the floor — ~$4,000+ for the plumbing move alone. The classic 5×8 three-fixture layout exists because it works.
  • Choose a tub-shower combo over a tiled walk-in. $2,500 installed vs. $6,000+ — and in a home's only full bath, the tub protects resale.
  • Pick porcelain over natural stone. Porcelain ($14/sq ft installed) mimics marble at a third of stone's price ($22/sq ft) with zero sealing maintenance.
  • Refinish instead of replacing the tub. Professional tub refinishing runs $400–$600 vs. $2,500 for a new combo — if the tub is sound, reglazing buys a decade.
  • Keep the vanity boxes, replace the top and hardware. A new quartz top and soft-close hardware transform a dated vanity for hundreds, not $1,800.
  • Bid in winter, pay in milestones. Contractors discount in slow months, and a milestone payment schedule (never more than 10–15% up front) keeps leverage on your side.

How your region moves the total

The calculator multiplies labor by your region: rural 0.85×, South 1.0×, Midwest 0.95×, Northeast 1.15×, West Coast 1.2×. On the $13,298 worked example above, labor is $3,448 in the South — the same job prices at ~$2,930 in a rural market and ~$4,140 on the West Coast. Fixtures and toilets are nationally priced, so the regional swing lands almost entirely on the labor and tile-setting lines. If a bid's labor share looks wildly different from these multipliers, ask why — it's a fast gouge detector.

Bathroom line items people forget to budget

The big fixtures get all the attention, but these smaller lines add up fast. A shower door — frameless glass runs $900–$2,000 installed and must be measured AFTER tile is set. An exhaust fan vented to the outside ($150–$400) prevents the mold that destroys bathroom drywall. Waterproofing behind the tile (membrane or backer board, $300–$800) is invisible but non-negotiable — skip it and you'll remodel twice. Grab bars and blocking ($100–$300) are cheap during a gut, expensive later. A heated floor mat ($600–$1,200) is the luxury line item with the highest satisfaction per dollar in cold climates. And permits ($200–$600) — most full remodels need one, and unpermitted plumbing can void insurance claims (October 2026 planning estimates).

Bathroom remodel cost FAQs

How much does a bathroom remodel cost in 2026?

Most US bathroom remodels land between $6,000 and $35,000. A cosmetic refresh runs $3,000–$8,000, mid-range $12,000–$25,000, major $20,000–$45,000, luxury $35,000+. Punch your size and fixtures into the calculator above.

What costs the most in a bathroom remodel?

Tile work plus the tub/shower — roughly 35–40% of a typical budget. Then labor (~25%), plumbing/electrical (~15%), and the vanity (~10%). The calculator shows every line item so nothing hides.

How much does a 5x8 bathroom remodel cost?

A standard 5×8 hall bath (40 sq ft) runs roughly $12,000–$25,000 for a mid-range full remodel — about $300–$625 per sq ft. A cosmetic refresh can be done for $3,000–$8,000. Our 5×8 bathroom guide has the full budget breakdown.

Is a walk-in shower cheaper than a tub?

A prefab tub-shower combo ($2,500 installed) is cheaper than a tiled walk-in shower ($6,000+). But removing a home's only tub can hurt resale — families with small children want one. In a master bath with a second tub elsewhere, the walk-in usually wins.

How accurate is this calculator?

It's a budgeting estimate built on October 2026 US planning prices — good for budgeting and sanity-checking bids. Results carry a ±25% planning range because quotes move with region, layout changes, and what's behind the walls. See how we compute costs for the full method.

How long does a bathroom remodel take?

A cosmetic refresh: 1–2 weeks. A mid-range full remodel: 2–4 weeks. A major remodel with layout changes: 4–8 weeks, plus 2–4 weeks of planning and permitting before demo starts. Tile setting and custom glass are the usual bottlenecks — order the shower door after tile is set.

What's the ROI of a bathroom remodel?

Mid-range bathroom remodels recoup roughly 60–70% at resale — among the best returns of any room. Keep it clean and mainstream: white fixtures, neutral tile, good lighting. Bold colors and ultra-trendy tile date fast and narrow your buyer pool.

Should I move the plumbing in my bathroom remodel?

Only if the layout is genuinely broken. Moving a toilet means rerouting a 3–4 inch drain line through the floor — budget $4,000+ for the move alone, plus the flooring and subfloor repair around it. In a 5×8 bath, better fixtures and lighting fix most complaints for far less.
