What's included — and what isn't

✓ Included in every estimate

  • Paint, per your brand, price, and coats
  • Primer, when your surface condition calls for it
  • Supplies allowance (tape, rollers, drop cloths)
  • Pro labor, when you select "Hire a pro"
  • 10% waste & touch-up allowance, rounded up to whole gallons

✗ Not included

  • Drywall repair beyond minor patching
  • Lead-paint testing or remediation (pre-1978 homes)
  • Moving or storing furniture
  • Wallpaper removal, color consultation, design fees
  • Scaffolding/lift rental on multi-story exteriors (often billed separately)

If a contractor's quote covers items on the right-hand list and ours doesn't, that's expected — it's also exactly what you should ask them to itemize before comparing.

How to measure a room in 2 minutes

  1. Measure the perimeter. Walk the room with a tape: length and width of each wall, or just the room's length × width — the calculator does 2×(L+W)×height for you.
  2. Use 8 ft unless you know better. Standard US ceilings are 8 ft; 9 ft in many newer homes. Vaulted ceilings need the average height.
  3. Count doors and windows. The calculator subtracts standard openings (21 sq ft per door, 15 per window) — close enough for estimating; pros measure exactly.
  4. Round paint up, never down. Running out mid-wall means a visible lap line and a second trip. The calculator's 10% waste allowance + round-up handles this.

Three ways people get the math wrong

  • Using floor square footage. A 12×15 room is 180 sq ft of floor but ~400 sq ft of wall. Quotes and calculators that don't specify which "square foot" they mean aren't comparable.
  • One coat fantasies. "One-coat coverage" is a marketing claim tested under lab conditions. Budget two coats; celebrate if one suffices.
  • Comparing price per gallon. A $74 gallon covering 425 sq ft can be cheaper per finished square foot than a $44 gallon covering 350. See our brand comparison for the real metric.

Paint cost FAQs

How many gallons of paint do I need for a 12×12 room?

About 384 sq ft of wall after subtracting a door and window. At 400 sq ft per gallon and 2 coats, that's ~1.9 gallons — buy 2, or 3 with a waste allowance. Punch your exact dimensions into the calculator above.

Do I need primer before painting?

Yes for new drywall, bare wood, or dramatic color changes (dark to light). Usually no when repainting a similar color over sound paint — especially with a paint-and-primer-in-one. The calculator auto-selects primer based on the condition you pick.

How much does it cost to hire a painter vs. DIY?

Pros charge $1–$6 per sq ft of paintable area in 2026 (or $25–$75/hour); labor is typically 70–85% of a pro job's total. DIY for a 12×15 room runs roughly $150–$300 in paint and supplies. Toggle "Hire a pro" in the calculator to see both.

Is expensive paint worth it?

Often, measured correctly. Premium paints cover more square feet per gallon and hide in fewer coats, so cost per finished square foot can nearly match mid-range paint. Our Behr vs. Sherwin-Williams vs. Benjamin Moore comparison does this math with September 2026 shelf prices.

How accurate is this calculator?

It's a budgeting estimate built on verified September 2026 prices and manufacturer coverage specs — good for budgeting and sanity-checking bids.
