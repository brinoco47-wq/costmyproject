What's included — and what isn't

✓ Included in every estimate

  • Siding materials per sqft of wall: panels/boards/bricks, fasteners, basic trim stock
  • Pro install labor per sqft, with stories adjustments, when you select "Hire a pro"
  • Wall-area conversion: 2 × (L + W) × 9 ft per story (planning convention)
  • Old-siding tear-off & disposal, when you select it
  • House wrap upgrade, trim/soffit/fascia package, and permit, when you add them

✗ Not included

  • Sheathing repair for rotted plywood/OSB found at tear-off (~$75–$100/sheet installed)
  • Structural work for full brick (veneer needs no footing; full brick does)
  • New windows/doors installed during the siding job
  • Painting for primed-only products (add ~$2–$4/sqft if not prefinished)
  • Landscaping repair around the foundation after scaffolding

If a contractor's bid covers items on the right-hand list and ours doesn't, ask them to itemize — that's exactly how you compare bids honestly.

Three ways people get the math wrong

  • Pricing floor area instead of wall area. A 2,000-sqft-footprint two-story home has ~3,240 sqft of wall — 62% more than the floor plan. Quotes that use "house square footage" without converting to wall area are the #1 reason per-square-foot numbers disagree across the web.
  • Forgetting tear-off and wrap. Stripping old siding (~$1/sqft) plus new house wrap (~$0.75/sqft) adds ~$5,670 on a 3,240-sqft wall — roughly 15% of a fiber-cement job. "New siding $25,000" quotes that skip both aren't comparable to ones that include them.
  • Comparing material prices instead of installed prices. Fiber cement panels at $4/sqft sound like 1.5× vinyl at $2.75 — but installed it's $9.50 vs. $5.25/sqft, because fiber cement's specialized labor (silica-safe cutting, heavy panels) widens the gap. Always compare installed cost per square foot, then divide by lifespan years for the honest number.

Siding cost FAQs

How much does house siding cost per square foot in 2026?

Installed, per square foot of wall: vinyl $3.50–$7.50, engineered wood $5.50–$10, fiber cement $7–$12, cedar/wood $8–$14, brick veneer $9.50–$18. Tear-off, wrap, trim, stories, and permits add on top — punch your numbers into the calculator above.

How do I calculate my home's wall square footage for siding?

Multiply the perimeter by wall height: 2 × (length + width) × 9 ft per story. A 40×50 two-story home is 2 × 90 × 9 × 2 = 3,240 sqft of wall. Window and door cutouts roughly offset gables, corners, and cutting waste — contractors use the same planning convention.

Is fiber cement siding worth the extra cost over vinyl?

It depends what you value. Vinyl wins on pure cost — roughly 40–45% cheaper over 25 years with almost zero maintenance. Fiber cement wins on fire rating (Class A non-combustible), impact resistance, and resale value (70–85% ROI reported). See our vinyl vs. fiber cement head-to-head.

Can I install siding myself to save money?

Vinyl is the most DIY-friendly siding — lightweight panels, interlocking system, forgiving cuts. Fiber cement is pro-only for most homeowners: silica dust is a health hazard when cutting, panels weigh ~2.5 lbs/sqft, and flashing details are unforgiving. Brick veneer is masonry work. Toggle DIY/pro above to see both numbers.

What adds the most to a siding quote besides the material?

Old-siding tear-off (~$1/sqft), house wrap (~$0.75/sqft), and the trim/soffit/fascia package ($1,000–$3,000) together can add 30–40% on top of the siding-materials number. Multi-story homes add ~10–20% labor for staging. Always compare itemized quotes, not lump sums.

How accurate is this calculator?

It's a budgeting estimate built on October 2026 installed-price ranges and pro labor data — good for budgeting and sanity-checking bids. Every result shows a ±25% planning range because region, stories, access, sheathing condition, and trim complexity move real quotes. See how we compute costs for the full method and limitations.
