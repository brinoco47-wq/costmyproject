What's included — and what isn't

✓ Included in every estimate

  • Roofing materials per sqft of roof surface: shingles/panels/tiles, underlayment, fasteners
  • Pro install labor per sqft, with pitch and stories adjustments, when you select "Hire a pro"
  • Pitch conversion: footprint × 1.031–1.414 to true roof surface (pure geometry)
  • Tear-off & disposal per layer, when you select it
  • Ice-and-water shield upgrade, skylight re-flashing, and permit, when you add them

✗ Not included

  • Decking replacement for rotted plywood/OSB (~$85/sheet installed, found at tear-off)
  • Structural reinforcement for heavy tile on homes not built for it
  • Gutters and downspouts ($5–$14 per linear foot)
  • Solar panel removal/reinstall (~$1,500–$3,000)
  • Interior repair for existing leak damage

If a contractor's bid covers items on the right-hand list and ours doesn't, ask them to itemize — that's exactly how you compare bids honestly.

Three ways people get the math wrong

  • Pricing the footprint instead of the roof. A 2,000-sqft home at 6/12 pitch has 2,236 sqft of roof — 12% more than the floor plan. At 12/12 it's 41% more. Quotes that skip the pitch factor are the #1 reason "per square foot" numbers disagree across the web.
  • Forgetting tear-off. Stripping one layer of old shingles runs ~$1.50/sqft — on a 2,236-sqft roof that's $3,354, nearly 20% of an architectural-shingle job. "New roof $8,000" quotes that skip tear-off aren't comparable to ones that include it.
  • Comparing material prices instead of installed prices. Metal panels at $6.75/sqft sound like 2.7× architectural shingles at $2.50 — but installed it's $11.75 vs. $6.00/sqft, because metal's specialized labor narrows the gap. Always compare installed cost per square foot, then divide by lifespan years for the honest number.

Roof cost FAQs

How much does a roof cost per square foot in 2026?

Installed, per square foot of roof surface: 3-tab asphalt $3.50–$5.50, architectural asphalt $4.50–$7.50, standing-seam metal $10–$16, wood shake $8.50–$14, clay/concrete tile $11–$18. Tear-off, pitch, stories, and permits add on top — punch your numbers into the calculator above.

How do I calculate my roof's square footage from my home's footprint?

Multiply footprint (length × width) by a pitch factor: 1.031 for 3/12, 1.118 for 6/12, 1.250 for 9/12, 1.414 for 12/12. The factor is the hypotenuse of the slope — pure geometry. A 2,000-sqft footprint at 6/12 is 2,236 sqft of roof, or about 22.4 roofing "squares" (100 sqft each).

Is a metal roof worth the extra cost over asphalt shingles?

Per year of service, yes. A 2,000-sqft-footprint architectural roof costs ~$17,120 installed with a ~27.5-year life (~$623/yr); standing-seam metal costs ~$33,890 with a ~55-year life (~$616/yr). Metal costs ~98% more upfront but is cheaper per year from day one — see our 30-year head-to-head.

How much does roof pitch change the price?

Two ways. Steeper pitch means more surface (a 12/12 roof has 41% more area than its footprint), and crews charge 15–25% more labor on steep pitches for safety gear and slower work. Toggle your pitch in the calculator — after material, it's the biggest cost lever.

Can I install a new roof myself to save money?

Technically possible, strongly discouraged. Labor is 40–65% of a roof quote, so the savings look tempting — but falls are a leading cause of construction deaths, most manufacturer warranties require professional installation, and a bad install voids both. Toggle DIY/pro above to see both numbers, then hire a pro.

How accurate is this calculator?

It's a budgeting estimate built on October 2026 installed-price ranges and pro labor data — good for budgeting and sanity-checking bids.
