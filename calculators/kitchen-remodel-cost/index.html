How the math works

  • Cabinets: linear feet ≈ kitchen sq ft × 0.25 (min 20 lf). Reface is a flat $4,000–$8,000; stock $100–$200/lf; semi-custom $200–$400/lf; custom $400–$800/lf. Midpoints used, then your exact size scales them.
  • Countertops: countertop area ≈ kitchen sq ft × 0.5, installed per sq ft — laminate $22, butcher block $70, granite $85, quartz $100, marble $135.
  • Flooring: kitchen sq ft × installed rate — vinyl $5.50, laminate $5.50, tile $12, hardwood $18 per sq ft.
  • Appliances: basic suite $3,000, mid-range suite $5,500, pro/luxury suite $15,000.
  • Plumbing / electrical / fixtures: flat allowance by scope — refresh $1,000, mid-range $3,000, major $6,000, luxury $12,000. Layout change adds $5,000.
  • Labor: 25% (refresh) / 30% (mid) / 35% (major) / 40% (luxury) of the material subtotal, multiplied by your region rate (0.85–1.2).

Every figure is an October 2026 US planning estimate. Real quotes vary — that's why results show a ±25% planning range. This is a budgeting estimate, not a quote.

Kitchen remodel cost FAQs

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in 2026?

Most US kitchen remodels land between $15,000 and $75,000. A cosmetic refresh runs $8,000–$18,000, mid-range $25,000–$60,000, major $60,000–$130,000, luxury $100,000+. Punch your size and finishes into the calculator above.

What costs the most in a kitchen remodel?

Cabinets — roughly 30% of a typical budget. Then labor (~20%), appliances (~15%), countertops (~10%), and flooring (~7%). The calculator shows every line item so nothing hides.

Is refacing cabinets cheaper than replacing?

Yes — refacing runs roughly $4,000–$8,000 versus $5,000–$20,000+ for new cabinets. It only works if the boxes are sound and you like the current layout. Our cabinet cost guide compares all four options.

How much do kitchen countertops cost per square foot?

Installed 2026 prices: laminate $15–$30, butcher block $40–$100, granite $50–$120, quartz $60–$140, marble $75–$200 per sq ft. See the countertop cost guide for edge profiles and the full comparison.

How accurate is this calculator?

It's a budgeting estimate built on October 2026 US planning prices — good for budgeting and sanity-checking bids. Results carry a ±25% planning range because quotes move with region, layout changes, and structural surprises. See how we compute costs for the full method.
